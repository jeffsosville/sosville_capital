Our process

How John runs a competitive sale

01 / Normalize

Build defensible adjusted EBITDA

Trailing-twelve-month numbers from processor data, with interest, owner costs and one-time items added back, so buyers bid on the true earnings.

02 / Package

A CIM and data room buyers trust

Location agreements, equipment, transaction history and terms organized before launch, so diligence questions don't stall momentum.

03 / Compete

Many qualified bidders, one timeline

Confidential outreach to strategic operators, ISOs, private equity, family offices and SBA-backed buyers, with proof of funds required and LOI deadlines enforced.

04 / Close

Counter, redline, transfer

Counter-proposals, LOI redlines, holdback terms and location-agreement transfers managed through to funding.

After-tax value

The headline price is half the job. What you keep is the other half.

We structure the deal alongside your CPA so more of the purchase price is taxed at the most favorable rate available to you.

Purchase-price allocation

Value weighted toward goodwill and location contracts, which generally qualify for capital-gains treatment, rather than equipment subject to depreciation recapture.

Asset vs. entity sale

Choosing the structure that fits your entity type and basis, negotiated up front in the LOI, not discovered at closing.

Timing the gain

Seller notes, installment terms and holdbacks used deliberately, so the gain can be spread across tax years where it helps.

Sosville Capital does not provide tax or legal advice. Structure is set with your own CPA and attorney.
